product

## Users

Network and systems administrators maintaining IP address pools in offline or restricted environments. They need to register addresses repeatedly, compare subnet utilization, search device details, and move data through CSV/XLSX or JSON backups.

## Product Purpose

IPViewer is a local-first IP address management tool for manual registration. It turns subnets into a compact, scannable grid while keeping editing, status changes, import/export, and recovery close at hand. Success means an operator can identify an address, update its record, and trust that the result is visible and recoverable without a backend or network dependency.

## Brand Personality

Quiet, dependable, precise. The interface should feel like a well-made operations console: dense enough for repeated work, calm enough for long sessions, and explicit about destructive or irreversible actions.

## Anti-references

Avoid marketing-page composition, decorative hero treatments, dashboard card forests, excessive gradients, oversized typography, playful illustration, and animated UI that delays or distracts from registration work. Avoid hiding important state in hover-only affordances when a clear status or action label is needed.

## Design Principles

- Make subnet state scannable before asking the operator to open a detail view.
- Use one restrained visual vocabulary for controls, forms, menus, dialogs, and feedback.
- Make destructive actions and data-loss risks explicit, reversible where possible, and easy to understand.
- Keep dense workflows keyboard-friendly and stable while preserving clear focus and selection states.
- Treat offline persistence and backup as first-class product behavior, not secondary settings.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Preserve keyboard navigation, visible focus states, readable contrast, text labels for compact controls, and reduced-motion behavior. Do not rely on color alone for subnet status; retain labels, tooltips, or text state indicators alongside semantic colors.
